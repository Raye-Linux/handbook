# Installing Raye Linux

You need a USB stick of at least 8 GB and 25 GB free on the computer.
Everything on the stick will be erased.

## 1. Make the stick

Download the Raye image (the `.iso` file) and write it to the stick with
a tool such as balenaEtcher or Rufus ("DD image" mode). Copying the file
by hand is not enough: the tool makes the stick bootable.

## 2. Start from the stick

Plug the stick in, restart, and open your computer's boot menu. The key
depends on the brand: often F12 (Dell, Lenovo), F9 (HP), F8 (ASUS) or
Esc. Pick the USB stick.

Raye starts with Secure Boot on, there is nothing to change in the
computer's settings.

## 3. Try or install

A window lets you **try** Raye without touching the computer, or
**install** it. Trying is a good way to check Wi-Fi, sound and the screen
before you decide.

## 4. The installer, step by step

1. **Wi-Fi**: if the computer has no cable, pick your network. Raye uses
   it to set the clock by itself.
2. **Language** and **keyboard**.
3. **Time zone**: already filled in when you are online.
4. **Disk**: "Erase disk" puts Raye alone on the computer. Tick
   "Encrypt" to protect your files with a password if the computer is
   stolen.
5. **Your account**: your name, a login and a password.

It takes about ten minutes. When it is done, restart: the computer goes
straight to Raye, even with the stick still in. You can take it out.
